Planning a spring event

Spring dates in Perth go quickly, and most venues make you compromise on something.

You want a room that fits the numbers properly, not one that is too tight at 40 or echoing at 80. You want food your guests will actually talk about, not a function menu. And you would rather make one phone call than manage a venue, a caterer, a hotel and an AV hire separately.

It is also the one event in the year that is quietly judged: by the board, by the client, by the family. Getting it right matters, and the best dates do not wait for the paperwork.

Why State Buildings

One heritage precinct. Fifteen spaces. One team that handles all of it.

State Buildings is not a function centre with a kitchen bolted on. It is a city block of restored 1800s heritage rooms on the corner of St Georges Terrace and Barrack Street, and the restaurants people already book for their own dinners, Post, Petition, Long Chim and Wildflower, are the kitchens that cater your event.

  • The right room for your number. Executive boardrooms and private dining rooms, The Mark for up to 60, and the Postal Hall for 160 seated or 300 cocktail.
  • Restaurant food, not function food. Menus are built with you, from canapes to a multi-course seated dinner, using the precinct's own chefs.
  • Everything under one roof. COMO The Treasury hotel sits above the spaces, so interstate guests and speakers stay where the event is.
  • One dedicated events coordinator from first site tour to the night itself.
