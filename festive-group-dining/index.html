Festive Group Dining at State Buildings

Four Restaurants, One Festive Season

At the heart of the State Buildings, festive gatherings take shape over generous feasts at Long Chim, Petition, Post and Wildflower, with menus made for sharing, celebrating and bringing people together. Please note, menus are subject to change.

Festive Group Dining at Long Chim

In the basement of State Buildings, Long Chim brings the festive season to life with the Maa Long Chim. This shared-style menu is available for lunch or dinner, with two menu options offered at lunch and one for dinner.

Festive Group Dining at Post

At Post, the festive season comes to the table with a choice of dining experiences for lunch or dinner. Settle into the bustling Main Dining Room for a four-course Festive Degustation, or choose from two menus in Post's private dining rooms: a three-course menu, or the Italian Table, a four-course experience featuring antipasto, shared entrée, pasta and 700g rib eye. Sparkling wine and oysters are also available to elevate the occasion.

Menu subject to change.

Festive Group Dining at Wildflower

Set high above the city, Wildflower's festive group dining menus are ideal for corporate groups and end-of-year celebrations. Lunch is served across three, six or eight courses, or an à la carte offering for parties up to eight guests, while dinner unfolds across four, six or eight courses. The Wildflower Private Dining Room is available upon request for nine guests or more.

Menu subject to change.
